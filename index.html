Research

What I work on

MS thesis · NUST, BIOMISA Research Group · completed June 2026

Seg-ITAML: An Incremental Task-Aware Meta-Learning Framework for Semantic Segmentation in Baggage Threat Detection

Airport scanners meet new prohibited items over time. Retraining a segmentation model from scratch for every new item is slow, and fine-tuning on the new item alone makes the model forget the old ones.

The thesis takes the idea behind iTAML from image classification to pixel-level segmentation. The resulting method, TAMIS, treats each new threat class as a task and learns one shared initialisation that a few gradient steps can specialise to any learned task. A small replay memory limits forgetting.

No task label is given at test time. TAMIS predicts the task itself, adapts on the stored examples of that task, and restricts its output to that task's classes.

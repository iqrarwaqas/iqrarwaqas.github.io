TAMIS: A Task-Aware Meta-Learning Framework for Class-Incremental Segmentation of X-ray Baggage Threats

Waqas Iqrar, Muhammad Usman Akram, Taimur Hassan, Kiran Khurshid, Mashood Mohammad Mohsan

Treats each incremental session as a task and combines Reptile-style meta-learning with a small replay memory. At test time the model predicts the task itself. Evaluated on PIDray, STCray, PIXray, SIXray and GDXray with a SegFormer-B2 backbone.

The AIoT Ecosystem for Next-Generation Satellite Systems

Waqas Iqrar, Kiran Khurshid, Sagheer Khan, Nasir Saeed

Physical Communication, vol. 74, 102967

A review of about 100 recent papers that proposes a unified taxonomy of AIoT in satellite systems, covering ground station scheduling, network optimisation, predictive maintenance and security.
